**Gap caliber.** Every gap above is the maximum interval between CONSECUTIVE events
of that timeline. It does **not** bound the interval before the first event (boot,
first data fetch) or after the last (final teardown), so it is not a whole-run
stall bound; the whole-run wall time and the per-window no-progress intervals below
are the complementary view. A step START line alone proves nothing about completion;
the execution-end timeline is the completion evidence, and the optimizer iteration
count equals its count (8) — the 15 serving weight-sync events are a different,
pre-existing chain the criterion requires to stay healthy, not a step count.
